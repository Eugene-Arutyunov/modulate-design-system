Modulate Fingerprint

The Modulate fingerprint is the house data visualization for conversations. The X axis is a timeline that operates on fragments of speech — clips — rather than raw audio; the Y axis discretely splits the picture by speaker, one lane per voice. The standard encoding is color for detected emotions and a glyph for detected behaviors.

The base format and its derivatives

The base format flexes within fixed ranges: it can be combined with the player or stand alone; the number of speakers can be 1, 2 or N, each on its own lane; it scales from an icon to a miniature to the full view; and it can show bare clips, clips with emotions, or emotions with behaviors.

Derived formats appear when the rules bend further: color encodes another data layer instead of emotions (synthetic voice, AI music); the audio is split into segments other than utterances, including equal windows; one speaker's clips are glued into a speak-time bar; or the Y axis doubles as amplitude, imitating a waveform.

It is important to understand that these derived formats cannot serve as the basis of recognizable brand imagery. They exist to solve specific tasks — when an idea tied to a particular model needs to be visualized, and needs to be visualized the Modulate way.

Scale

Scale is a narrative rather than a control. The same language reads as an icon, as a miniature in a table, and at full size in the player.
