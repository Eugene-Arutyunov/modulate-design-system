Why You Can’t Seriously Say “I’m Not a Designer”

Abstract

If you make things, you’re a designer — the only question is whether you’re a good one. Caring in a thousand small decisions is why the Modulate design system is now public.

Everyone is a designer

You designed something today. Maybe it was a slide for a team meeting. A vibe-coded app. The printed sign for your garage sale.

If you made something — anything — you designed it.

And here’s the uncomfortable part: if it looks bad, you don’t get to say “well, I’m not a designer.” You are. You’re just a bad one right now.

Design is not a job title

Design is a discipline. A craft. And like any craft, it lives in a thousand small decisions: this spacing or that one, this word or a shorter one — or an image instead. The default Google template or a custom one. These are all design decisions.

Doing good design and spending extra time on it is a choice.

Some people will tell you: who cares? If it works, it’s good. Pixel-perfection is for nerds. Better design won’t make you more money tomorrow.

And here’s another uncomfortable truth: they might be right. Not tomorrow.

But I’m convinced that caring pays off. People notice who cares — and they look for them. Loyalty can’t be bought; it can only be earned. Slowly. Consistently. Over years. And we believe that, in the end, it’s the most valuable thing a company has.

I want to live in a world where people care about what they make and are proud of it — where “good enough” isn’t the default and craft isn’t a luxury. In the time of AI slop I feel it stronger than ever.

I’m lucky: at Modulate I found people who care the same way.

That’s why we’re opening our design system

Today we’re making the Modulate Design System public — as an open repository on Modulate’s GitHub.

We’ve spent many months building it: tokens, components, patterns, principles — the whole thing. It’s how we make sure that caring scales, that quality isn’t a happy accident but a system. A team that sweats the details of a button will sweat the details of everything else. Quality design and a quality product are the same habit.

To be transparent about the scope: right now the system is built primarily for interfaces and vibe-coding. Feed it to Claude and get clean, consistent UI out of the box. Presentations, marketing materials, social media — that’s where we want to take it next.

We could have kept it internal. Most companies do. But if design is about caring, then sharing what we’ve learned is part of the craft.

So: take it. Use it. Fork it. Change whatever you want — it’s yours now too.

And we’re starting to write

Alongside the system, we’re opening a blog about good design and the reasoning behind our decisions — not just what we chose, but why. Eugene Arutyunov, our Design lead and evangelist, has already written about page composition, color, icons, and why the main axis of any layout is time, not X and Y. The trade-offs, the mistakes, the details most people never see.

If you’re someone who cares about craft — or wants to know more — follow us on LinkedIn and build alongside us.

Ilya Sinelnikov, VP of Product & Design at Modulate

P.S. Yes, I know, “Design” is literally in my job title. So no excuses for me.
